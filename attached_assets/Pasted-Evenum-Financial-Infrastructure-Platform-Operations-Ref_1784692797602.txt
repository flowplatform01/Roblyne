Evenum — Financial Infrastructure & Platform Operations Refinement
Context
This prompt is an incremental refinement to the existing project.
Read the original master prompt completely.
Read every previous refinement prompt completely.
Do not rebuild the application.
Do not remove existing functionality.
Continue evolving the current production codebase while preserving architecture, improving quality, and filling the remaining platform gaps.
The objective of this refinement is to transform Evenum from an application that supports participation into a platform that can professionally operate a business.

Mission
The current implementation already supports voting, events, ticketing, campaigns, organizations, and public participation.
However, the platform still lacks a complete business operations layer.
This refinement focuses on introducing professional financial infrastructure, commission management, settlement workflows, administrative operations, and business intelligence.
This is not about adding unnecessary features.
It is about completing the platform's operational backbone.

Revenue Philosophy
Do not introduce subscriptions, premium plans, advertisements, verification fees, or unrelated monetization models.
For now, Evenum earns revenue only through platform commissions collected from supported financial activities.
Revenue must be generated transparently and automatically through the platform.
Participants should never feel that the platform exists primarily to charge them.
Organizations remain the customers.
Participants remain the users.
The platform earns by facilitating trusted professional participation.

Platform Commission Engine
Introduce a centralized Commission Engine.
No pillar should calculate commissions independently.
Every financial transaction must pass through this service.
Supported transaction types include:
* Paid voting
* Ticket purchases
* Campaign contributions
* Registration payments
* Any future paid participation experience
The Commission Engine should:
* Calculate platform commission
* Support percentage commissions
* Support fixed commissions where needed
* Calculate organization earnings
* Produce financial records
* Generate settlement records
* Generate audit records
* Remain reusable across every pillar
This engine becomes the single source of truth for all financial calculations.

Platform Financial Ledger
Every financial activity must be permanently recorded.
The ledger should capture:
* Transaction identifier
* Organization
* Related pillar
* Related entity
* Gross amount
* Platform commission
* Organization earnings
* Currency
* Status
* Payment provider
* Settlement status
* Timestamp
* Audit history
Financial records must never disappear.
Every movement must remain traceable.

Organization Financial Workspace
Each organization should have a dedicated financial workspace.
This is not just a balance.
It should include:
* Current available balance
* Pending balance
* Total earnings
* Total commissions paid
* Settlement history
* Transaction history
* Exportable financial reports
* Payment history
* Campaign earnings
* Event earnings
* Voting earnings
* Revenue analytics
Organizations should clearly understand how money moves through their account.

Settlement Workflow
Revenue should follow a structured lifecycle.
Collected
↓
Pending Settlement
↓
Ready for Settlement
↓
Released
↓
Completed
Administrators should have visibility into this pipeline.
Organizations should always understand the current settlement state.
Settlement logic should remain extensible for future payment providers.

Commission Configuration
Platform administrators should configure commission policies without modifying code.
Configuration should support:
* Global commission rate
* Voting commission
* Event ticket commission
* Campaign commission
* Registration commission
* Currency-aware configuration
* Future extensibility
Do not hardcode financial percentages anywhere.
All financial policies should be centrally managed.

Business Operations Module
Platform Administration requires a dedicated Business Operations section.
This becomes the operational heart of Evenum.
Include areas for:
Revenue Overview
Commission Management
Financial Transactions
Settlement Management
Organization Balances
Payment Monitoring
Refund Management Architecture
Financial Reports
Operational Metrics
Audit History
This area should feel like software used to operate a company, not merely observe statistics.

Create executive-level dashboards.

Present this information professionally.

These insights should help organizations improve outcomes rather than merely review data.

Platform administrators must be able to monitor platform-wide activity.
Support:
Organization health
Campaign activity
Event activity
Voting activity
Financial anomalies
Operational alerts
Platform growth
Recent activity
Business performance
This should extend existing administration rather than replace it.

Reporting & Export
Professional platforms require professional reporting.
Support exporting:
Financial summaries
Settlement reports
Revenue reports
Transaction reports
Organization reports
Campaign reports
Event reports
Voting reports
Exports should support common professional formats and preserve data integrity.

Architecture
Keep financial logic independent from presentation.
Separate:
Financial calculations
Commission policies
Settlement workflow
Ledger
Analytics
Reporting
Administrative interfaces
Public interfaces
Avoid coupling business rules directly to UI components.

User Experience
Financial interfaces should communicate trust.
Use clear terminology.
Present balances carefully.
Provide transparent calculations.
Avoid ambiguous wording.
Users should always understand:
* What they paid
* Why they paid
* Platform commission (where appropriate)
* Organization earnings
* Transaction status
* Settlement status
Transparency increases confidence.

Quality Expectations
Review every existing financial workflow.
Identify incomplete implementations.
Identify duplicated logic.
Identify inconsistent calculations.
Consolidate them into the new architecture.
Do not leave temporary implementations behind.
Do not create parallel financial systems.
There should be one financial engine powering every pillar.

Final Objective
This refinement should complete Evenum's operational business foundation.
By the end of this implementation:
* Every revenue-generating pillar should use a unified Commission Engine.
* Financial records should be auditable and traceable.
* Organizations should have a professional financial workspace.
* Platform administrators should have a complete Business Operations center.
* Revenue, commissions, settlements, and reporting should be treated as first-class platform capabilities.
* The implementation should remain production-ready, scalable, maintainable, and consistent with the overall Evenum architecture established in the previous prompts.
The goal is not to add more screens. The goal is to make Evenum operate like a real software company behind the scenes, with the same level of professionalism that users experience on the front end.
