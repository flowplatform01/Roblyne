EVENUM — FOUNDER REFLECTION, PRODUCT ASSESSMENT & PRE-LAUNCH SCORECARD

I want you to take a step back from your normal development work and conduct a thoughtful, honest assessment of EVENUM as a product.

This is not another feature-development task. It is not a request to redesign the application, perform an endless hardening exercise, or generate another list of things we could potentially build.

I want you to evaluate what we have actually accomplished.

You have access to the application and its codebase, so you are in a better position than someone evaluating EVENUM from a description alone. Use that access to understand the real product, its current capabilities, its architecture, its user experience, and its readiness for the next stage.

The goal is to help the founder step back, appreciate the work that has gone into EVENUM, understand its current strengths, identify its remaining uncertainties, and gain a clearer perspective on moving from development toward production and marketing.

This is a founder-level review. Be analytical, but also human, encouraging, and thoughtful.

Do not simply praise the product. Give praise where it is deserved, identify weaknesses where evidence supports them, and distinguish verified facts from assumptions.

A. The customer-facing side:
- What organizers can accomplish.
- What participants, voters, applicants, and attendees experience.

B. The operational and technical side:
- How the application is structured.
- How its major workflows are implemented.
- How data, permissions, and organizational boundaries are handled.
- How payments and other external integrations work.
- How the application is intended to operate in production.
- What evidence exists for reliability, performance, security, and maintainability.

Do not infer that a feature is complete merely because a page or button exists. Inspect its implementation and available tests. Where practical, verify important workflows.
