- Is it associated with the correct participant?
- Is the amount correct?

- Is the payment state distinguishable from a merely initiated transaction?

- Does the organization dashboard reflect it?

- Does the transaction history reflect it?
- Does a failed payment avoid being recorded as successful?
- Does a cancelled payment remain distinguishable?
- Does a pending payment remain pending?
- Can duplicate processing occur?

The objective is not to redesign the payment gateway.

The objective is to verify that Evenum's internal financial state is trustworthy.

If external payment integration is not currently active, do not invent fake production behaviour.

Instead verify that the internal architecture is correctly prepared for real payment events.

============================================================
18. OBSERVABILITY AND TRACEABILITY
============================================================

A major requirement:

When something fails, the team must be able to determine WHAT failed and WHERE it failed.

Do not accept a generic:

"Runtime Error"

without useful diagnostic context.

Review the application's error handling and observability architecture.

Where appropriate, errors should contain:

- unique error/reference ID
- timestamp
- operation/context
- route or feature
- environment
- correlation/request ID
- useful diagnostic metadata
- safe user-facing message

Sensitive information must never be exposed to end users.

The developer/operator side should have enough information to trace failures.

The objective is:

USER sees:
"Something went wrong. Please try again."

OPERATOR/ENGINEER can determine:
"Exactly what failed, where, under what operation, and why."
