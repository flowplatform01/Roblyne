
Evenum — Product Experience, Identity & Excellence Refinement
IMPORTANT

Read it completely.
Read the refinement prompt completely.
Do not replace either of them.
This prompt exists because the current implementation, while functional, still does not fully embody the vision of Evenum.
The application works.
The architecture is improving.
The features exist.
However, the overall product still feels like software that functions rather than a platform that inspires confidence.
This refinement is about elevating the product from functional to exceptional.

Your Mission
You are no longer implementing features.
You are acting as a world-class multidisciplinary product team preparing Evenum for public launch.
Challenge everything.
Do not assume existing implementations are correct simply because they work.
If something feels generic, redesign it.
If something feels incomplete, complete it.
If something feels ordinary, elevate it.
Do not stop at "it works."
Continue until the application genuinely feels premium.

Stop Thinking Like CRUD Software
One of the biggest remaining weaknesses is that many experiences still feel like traditional CRUD interfaces.
Creating.
Editing.
Listing.
Viewing.
Submitting.
That is not the product.
Those are merely operations.
Evenum is about participation.
Everything should revolve around participation experiences rather than management screens.
Management exists to support the experience—not replace it.

Build Experiences, Not Pages
Every public-facing interface should feel like entering a destination.
Never design pages as simple forms.
Never design them as information cards stacked together.
Instead, create immersive experiences.
Users should immediately understand:
* what this is
* why it matters
* who organized it
* what they are participating in
* why they should continue
The platform should create confidence before users perform any action.

Voting Must Feel Like a Real Election
This is currently the weakest area.
The current implementation behaves correctly but emotionally feels like completing a survey or poll.
That is fundamentally different from the vision.
Evenum is a professional voting platform.
A user should immediately feel that they have entered a real election.
Redesign the entire public voting experience around this principle.
Do not simply display positions and candidate cards.
Instead create a complete election experience.
Include rich election identity.
Professional election branding.
Election cover media.
Organization identity.
Election overview.
Election timeline.
Countdown while active.
Election announcements.
Participation statistics where appropriate.
Verified indicators where appropriate.
Election rules.
Election information.
Professional navigation through the election.
Allow users to understand the election before asking them to vote.
Candidates should never feel like plain records inside cards.
Candidates are contestants.
Present them with personality and professionalism.
Support rich profiles including:
Professional photography
Campaign branding
Manifesto
Biography
Vision
Promises
Experience
Media where appropriate
Comparison experience

Shareable candidate pages
Professional ballot presentation
Voting should feel deliberate.
Not mechanical.
Review and confirmation should reinforce trust before submission.
Success should feel meaningful.
After voting, users should feel they participated in an important event—not merely submitted a form.
Results should tell a story.
Do not only display tables.
Present participation.
Rankings.
Statistics.
Turnout.

Visual summaries where appropriate.
Professional presentation.
The entire voting lifecycle should communicate credibility.
